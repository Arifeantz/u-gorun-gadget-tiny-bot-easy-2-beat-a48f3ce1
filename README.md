A trend-filtered momentum rotation bot for Solana that beats buy-and-hold. It ranks FARTCOIN, JITOSOL, POPCAT, RAY, SOL, WETH and WIF by trailing 12-hour return, holds at most the top 4 names above +1%, and only invests when at least 3 of the 7 symbols are rising — otherwise it sits in cash. Positions are rebalanced every 8 hours, cut by a 10% stop-loss or when their 12-hour momentum turns below −5%, and sized evenly up to 25% of equity each. Deterministic: no clock, no randomness, no network calls.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
